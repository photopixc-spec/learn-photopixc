PhotoPixc Skill Lab 45 — CHEATSHEET 04: Mobile CRO (Day 26-30)

Customer journey: Ad → Landing → Product → Cart → Checkout → Confirmation.
Observe: navigation difficulty, speed, price clarity, delivery/returns, form fields, trust.
Evidence gathering: Person/Device (non-identifiable) | Task | Stopping point | Friction | Severity.
Hypothesis format: If we [change], then [behavior] may improve because [observed evidence].
Suggested KPIs: add_to_cart rate, begin_checkout rate, purchase conversion rate; define denominators.
Guardrails: refund rates, errors, complaints, accessibility.
Prioritization: expected impact, confidence, effort; no guaranteed uplift.
Before publishing: verify actual price, shipping fees and return policy.
Five usability interviews show problems, not definitive experiment results.
