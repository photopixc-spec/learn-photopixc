PhotoPixc Skill Lab 45 — CHEATSHEET 06: Offers & Portfolio (Day 38-45)

Offer page: Who it serves | Problem | Clear deliverables | Process | Timeline | Boundaries | CTA.
Onboarding: Client contact | Scope | Date range | Account permissions | KPIs | Review deadline.
Audit deliverable: Findings + Evidence + Risk + Priority + Recommendations + Unknowns.
Case Study: Context | Method | Observed findings | Actions (if authorized) | Verified outcome (if available).
Pricing: Deliverables + time + overhead + revision scope + risk; no fabricated market guarantees.
Portfolio: distinguish Simulation, Example, Authorized audit, Verified results.
Never publish client passwords or confidential campaign / customer data.
Service boundary: Read-only audit does not include editing live campaigns.
Outreach: short, specific, relevant, permission-based; no spam or invented results.
